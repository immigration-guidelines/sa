What exists
- HRSD knowledge centre, "Regulations and procedures":
  https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/knowledge-centre/decisions-and-regulations/regulation-and-procedures
  with sub-lists .../evidence (guides), .../procedure, .../regulation, .../system, .../violations.
  The "guides" list holds procedural guides (al-dalil al-ijra'i) for Saudisation decisions (sales,
  marketing, engineering, project management, dentistry, support-administrative occupations, etc.),
  Nitaqat, occupational-fitness examinations, and the "Guide to classifying work permits by skill
  category" (dalil tasnif rukhas al-'amal hasba al-fi'at al-maharia, 5 July 2025, implementing
  Ministerial Decision No. 4602 of 10/1/1447 H):
  https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2025-07/dlyl-tsnyf-rkhs-al-ml-bhsb-alfy-at-almharyt---alnskht-almhdwtht---1ywlyw-2025m.pdf
  Each guide's page names its audience as "employers, individuals" and states that the guide's purpose
  is to inform the labour market (establishments and non-Saudi workers). They are public information
  on how the decisions apply, not instructions to the officials who decide; not kept.
- The "procedure" list holds ministerial rules (e.g. rules on Saudi-staffing intermediation, domestic-
  worker contractual relations, job-advertisement rules) - legal acts, not wanted.
- No Jawazat manual, circular or instruction for officers was found (web searches, November 2026,
  returned only news reports and expatriate guides).

Finding
Saudi Arabia publishes no guidance addressed to the officials who decide residence or work-permit
cases. MHRSD publishes many employer-facing procedural guides and its ministerial decisions; Jawazat
publishes nothing beyond service announcements. Decisions are largely system-driven inside Qiwa,
Muqeem and Absher, whose rules are not public.
